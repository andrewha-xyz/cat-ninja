# UI — what you see and hear 🎨

**Owner:** the 10 y.o.

You own the blade trail, the score and lives, the start and game-over screens, and the sounds.

## Files

- `input.js` — follows your mouse or finger and draws the glowing blade
- `hud.js` — draws the score and hearts at the top
- `screens.js` — the start screen and the game-over screen
- `sound.js` — little beeps

## Tasks

Do them in order. Each one is a pull request.

1. **Your blade.** Change the trail color and glow. Make it thick in the middle and thin at the ends.
2. **Lives as cats.** Show 🐱 instead of ❤️, and flash the screen red for a moment when you lose one.
3. **Start screen.** Add how to play and your best score.
4. **Sounds.** Different sounds for the golden cat and the bomb. Press `M` to mute.
5. **Background.** A night sky with stars, or rooftops, or anything you like.
6. **+1 popups** (stretch). Show "+1" or "+5" floating up where a cat was sliced.
